## Evaluation scope

This candidate combines the reviewed Phase 1 foundation with Phase 2:

- versioned RF and subscriber inputs;
- source-aware elevation and reproducible HAAT;
- explainable band/environment planning estimates;
- separate talk-out, talk-in, and probable two-way estimates;
- immutable field observations, append-only review, and incident-local
  calibration; and
- retained P2.6 validation evidence from approved ICS 205 content through exact
  source/model/profile/calibration versions, deterministic synthetic
  comparisons, stale-data detection, controlled export, and audit
  verification.

The bundle records screening-only confidence, supported/unsupported conditions,
tested limits, sensitivity, actors, timestamps, application version, and
digests. It excludes ICS 205 contact fields and remarks plus observation
coordinates, observer/source text, and notes.

## Known limitations

- All repository and candidate evaluation data is synthetic.
- No result is a propagation study, field/scientific validation, frequency
  coordination decision, spectrum authorization, or coverage guarantee.
- Queue cancellation is supported; execution is an explicit synchronous
  request with no mid-request cancellation or background-worker recovery.
- ICS 205 assignments are not yet directly linked to RF profiles. The evidence
  reports that traceability gap instead of inventing a relationship.
- Local bounded tokens are not SSO or multifactor authentication.
- Performance evidence is a regression envelope, not a production capacity or
  service-level objective.
- External map, elevation, RadioReference, and other providers remain subject
  to their separate licensing, privacy, security, reliability, and human
  gates.
- Real observation/incident data remains prohibited until an explicit
  classification, retention, disclosure, and access decision is approved.
