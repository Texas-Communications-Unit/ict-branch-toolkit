## Evaluation scope

This candidate freezes the Phase 1 non-production prototype after the work
reviewed through PRs #9, #27, #29, #40, #41, #46, #48, and #52.

Included evaluation capabilities are:

- authenticated incidents, operational periods, and incident-scoped roles;
- versioned conventional-channel and talkgroup libraries with controlled
  reference provenance;
- guarded NIFOG 2.02 public-reference extraction and import support;
- revisioned ICS-205 planning, approval locks, audit history, and exports;
- map-based radio-site planning, coordinate conversion, manual rings, and
  spatial exports;
- secure headers, configurable HTTPS/HSTS behavior, throttling, generic error
  handling, bounded local-token lifecycle, and incident-scoped authorization;
- tested backup, checksum verification, isolated restore, upgrade, migration
  rollback, and application rollback procedures;
- audit abuse-case tests, representative regression envelopes, CodeQL,
  dependency/secret/container scanning, and CycloneDX SBOM generation.

The explainable RF deconfliction rule engine is not included. P1.4 was deferred
to issue #39 and must not be represented as a candidate capability.

## Known limitations and blocked uses

- The candidate accepts synthetic data only.
- Local database tokens are a bounded prototype design, not SSO or centralized
  account management.
- The local audit hash chain is tamper-evident within its documented trust
  boundary; it is not a signed remote ledger or SIEM archive.
- Published performance values are deterministic regression envelopes, not a
  production capacity rating or service-level objective.
- Deployment-specific PostgreSQL/PostGIS load and concurrency testing has not
  established an operational user or incident capacity.
- External map, geocoder, hosted provider, QGIS Server, and offline-map work
  requires separate privacy, licensing, reliability, and human approval.
- The explainable RF deconfliction engine remains deferred.
- Coverage and deconfliction output is planning decision support, not
  authorization, coordination approval, propagation study, or guarantee.
- No production-supported version or production service-level objective exists.
- Publication does not authorize deployment or non-synthetic data.
